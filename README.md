## Interactive Pitch Deck

The investor pitch deck (`nudgen-pitch-deck.html`) is fully self-contained with no external local dependencies. The logo is embedded as base64 data, and fonts are loaded dynamically from the Google Fonts CDN. You can open it in any modern web browser without a local web server.

### Slide Structure (10 Slides)

1. **Cover** — One-liner positioning, main logo, and hero statistics (Accenture Strategy customer churn study, Harvard Business Review customer retention vs acquisition costs, Grand View Research digital marketing software market).
2. **The Opportunity (Market Context)** — Highlighting the Australian Shopify opportunity with 130K active stores, average annual revenue, and lack of dedicated marketing staff.
3. **Problem** — Wasted hours on YouTube tutorials, flow builders, and segment logic for Mailchimp, Klaviyo, and HubSpot instead of running the store.
4. **Solution (How It Works)** — The 3-step automated retention workflow (Integrate → Detect → Automate) that personalizes, brands, and segments customer emails.
5. **Competition** — Positioning matrix highlighting Nudgen's low complexity and high automation compared to Mailchimp, Klaviyo, HubSpot, and Salesforce Marketing Cloud.
6. **Our Moat** — Real revenue attribution tying email performance directly to sales ($3,150 from 3 campaigns in the last 30 days) and weekly performance charts.
7. **Team** — Co-founders Nathan Nguyen (Data Scientist at Webjet.com.au) & Toan Nhu (Principal Software Engineer at VinGroup, ex Tiki).
8. **Traction** — Milestone timeline showing problem validation, private MVP beta (5 active Shopify stores, $3.15k revenue), public launch, and scaling.
9. **Business Model** — Clear pricing tiers (Startup at $99/mo, Growth at $299/mo) and excellent unit economics (CAC, LTV, Payback).
10. **Ask** — $250K pre-seed raise details (60% App Store launch & scaling, 40% automated integrations) with founder contact details.

## About Nudgen

Nudgen helps SaaS and Shopify businesses stop losing customers by automating personalized retention email workflows — triggered by real behavioral signals, not generic drip campaigns.

- **Website:** [nudgen.net](https://nudgen.net)
- **Founders:** Nathan Nguyen & Toan Nhu (Co-founders)
- **Design Language:** Sleek dark neutrals (`#0a0a0a`) with vibrant emerald accents (`#059669` / `#10b981`), Outfit (headings) + Inter (body) typography.
